# Opening Checklist

How to open the shop, in order. Start the pearls early: they cook and rest for about 30 min while you set up everything else.

Each photo sits under the step it shows. Tap a photo to see it bigger.

<div class="lsc-meta" markdown>
**Purpose:** Open the shop the same way every day, so everything is ready when the first customer walks in.<br>
**Who:** Opening staff<br>
**When:** Every day, as soon as you arrive at the shop (the shop opens at 11:30 am)<br>
**Time needed:** about 10 min (the pearls keep cooking and resting after that)<br>
**Equipment/supplies:** pearl cooker, kettle, bowl, strainer, red and green chopping boards, iPad, ice maker, air-con control<br>
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-07
</div>

## 1. Lights

1. Unlock the door and go in.
2. Turn **on all the lights** at the switches.

    [![Light switches on the wall](../assets/procedures/opening/01-light-switches.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/01-light-switches.jpg)

## 2. Start the pearls

!!! warning "⚠️ Warning: boiling water"
    The pearl cooker and the kettle get very hot. Keep your hands away from the steam when you open the lid.

1. Turn on the **power at the wall** for the pearl cooker.

    [![Turning on the pearl cooker at the wall](../assets/procedures/opening/02-pearl-cooker-power.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/02-pearl-cooker-power.jpg)

2. Fill the pot with water up to **line 0.5**.
3. Press the **hand button**.
4. Press the **red button** (start).

    [![Pearl cooker panel: hand button and red start button](../assets/procedures/opening/03-pearl-cooker-start.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/03-pearl-cooker-start.jpg)

5. Check that the **2 boxes at the top left** of the panel light up red.
6. Wait for the water to boil. The cooker **beeps** when it boils.

While the water heats up, measure the pearls:

1. Take a **bowl**.

    [![Bowl used to measure the pearls](../assets/procedures/opening/04-pearl-bowl.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/04-pearl-bowl.jpg)

2. Scoop the dry pearls from the pearl tub:
    - **Monday to Friday:** **1 full bowl**
    - **Saturday and Sunday:** **1.5 bowls**

    [![Tub of dry pearls and the measuring bowl](../assets/procedures/opening/05-pearl-tub.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/05-pearl-tub.jpg)

## 3. Put away the dishes

1. Put away everything on the **dish rack**.

    [![Dish rack with clean items](../assets/procedures/opening/06-dish-rack.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/06-dish-rack.jpg)

## 4. Set up the waffle and topping stations

1. Take **1 green chopping board** and **1 red chopping board**.
2. Set up the **waffle station** with the green board and the scissors, as in the photo.

    [![Waffle station with the green board and scissors](../assets/procedures/opening/07-waffle-station.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/07-waffle-station.jpg)

3. Set up the **topping station** with the red board and the knife, as in the photo.
4. Check that **all the sauces** are ready.

    [![Topping station with the red board, knife and sauces](../assets/procedures/opening/08-topping-station.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/08-topping-station.jpg)

## 5. Turn on the Nutella warmer

Nutella must be warmed up before it can be used.

1. Turn on the **power** for the Nutella warmer at the wall.

    [![Turning on the Nutella warmer at the wall](../assets/procedures/opening/09-nutella-warmer-power.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/09-nutella-warmer-power.jpg)

2. Check that the warmer's button shows **green**. Green means it is on.

    [![Nutella warmer with the green light on](../assets/procedures/opening/10-nutella-warmer-on.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/10-nutella-warmer-on.jpg)

## 6. Check the topping station

1. Check the topping trays. Make sure **everything is full** enough for the day.

    [![Topping tray, full](../assets/procedures/opening/11-topping-tray.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/11-topping-tray.jpg)

## 7. iPad, music and Uber Eats

1. Turn on the **iPad**.
2. Open **Spotify** and play **English music**.
3. Open **Uber Eats**.
4. Leave the iPad screen on, in its place.
5. Check that Uber Eats shows the shop as **Open**.

    [![iPad on its stand showing the Uber Eats Orders screen](../assets/procedures/opening/12-ipad-orders.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/12-ipad-orders.jpg)

## 8. Check the ice cream

1. Check the ice cream display. Make sure every tub is **full**.

    [![Ice cream display](../assets/procedures/opening/13-ice-cream-display.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/13-ice-cream-display.jpg)

2. If **vanilla** or **chocolate** is down to **half a tub**, top it up from the backup in the **freezer**.

## 9. Finish the pearls

1. When the water boils, add the measured pearls to the pot.
2. Stir gently with a spoon.
3. Close the lid. The cooker cooks the pearls by itself.
4. Wait until the screen shows **End**.
5. Turn off the power at the back of the cooker.
6. Leave the pearls to rest in the closed pot for **20 min**.
7. After 20 min, pour the pearls into the **strainer**.

    [![Strainer for the pearls, hanging on the wall](../assets/procedures/opening/14-pearl-strainer.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/14-pearl-strainer.jpg)

8. Rinse them with **cold water** for about **30 s**.
9. Pour the pearls into a clean **tub**.

    [![Clean tubs on the shelf](../assets/procedures/opening/15-tubs-shelf.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/15-tubs-shelf.jpg)

10. Add **hot water**.
11. Add **4 tbsp sugar**.

## 10. Ice maker

1. Check the ice. If the ice is gone, or there's **less than half**, start the ice maker:
2. Plug in the ice maker's cable.

    [![Plugging in the ice maker](../assets/procedures/opening/16-ice-maker-power.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/16-ice-maker-power.jpg)

3. Turn on the power.
4. Check that there is **water in the tank**. If not, pour water in.

    [![Opening the ice maker's water tank](../assets/procedures/opening/17-ice-maker-water.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/17-ice-maker-water.jpg)

5. The ice maker makes ice by itself.

## 11. Air conditioning

1. Press the **middle button** on the air-con control.
2. Set it to **24 °C**.

    [![Pressing the middle button on the air-con control](../assets/procedures/opening/18-air-conditioner.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/18-air-conditioner.jpg)

## 12. Bingsu station

1. Check the bingsu station for anything that needs topping up.
2. Make sure there are enough bingsu toppings for the day: red bean, almond, strawberries, chocolate cream and the rest.
3. Then check that there is **soufflé cream** in the fridge.

## 13. Batter and iced tea

1. Check the batter under the waffle station. There must always be:
    - **3 jugs** of original batter
    - **2 jugs** of chocolate batter

    [![Jugs of original and chocolate batter](../assets/procedures/opening/19-batter-jugs.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/19-batter-jugs.jpg)

2. If there isn't enough, take a **3 L jug**, fill it with the batter made in advance, and add it.
3. Check the **iced tea** in the fridge. There must always be **3 jugs of 5 L**. If there are only 2 left, make more.

    [![Fridge with the iced tea](../assets/procedures/opening/20-fridge-iced-tea.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/20-fridge-iced-tea.jpg)

## 14. Front of shop

1. Turn on the **lucky cat light** on the front counter.

    [![Lucky cat light on the front counter](../assets/procedures/opening/21-lucky-cat-light.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/21-lucky-cat-light.jpg)

2. Turn on the **unicorn light** with the switch on its cord, on the wall by the unicorn toys.

    [![Switch on the cord for the unicorn light](../assets/procedures/opening/22-unicorn-light-switch.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/22-unicorn-light-switch.jpg)

3. Check that the cat sign on the door shows **WE ARE OPEN**.

    [![Cat sign on the door showing WE ARE OPEN](../assets/procedures/opening/23-open-sign.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/23-open-sign.jpg)

4. Turn on the light at the front window with the **switch on the floor** under the window.

    [![Light switch on the floor under the front window](../assets/procedures/opening/24-floor-light-switch.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/24-floor-light-switch.jpg)
    [![Front window with the unicorn neon light](../assets/procedures/opening/25-front-window.jpg){ .lsc-step-photo loading=lazy }](../assets/procedures/opening/25-front-window.jpg)

5. Turn on **all the other lights** at the front. Every light in the shop must be on.

## Checklist

Print this and tick each item.

- [ ] All lights on
- [ ] Pearls cooked, rested 20 min, rinsed, in a tub with hot water and sugar
- [ ] Dish rack emptied
- [ ] Waffle station (green board) and topping station (red board) set up
- [ ] All sauces ready
- [ ] Nutella warmer on (green light)
- [ ] Topping trays full
- [ ] iPad on, music playing, Uber Eats **Open**
- [ ] Ice cream full; vanilla and chocolate topped up from the freezer if half
- [ ] Ice maker on, with water in the tank (if ice was below half)
- [ ] Air-con on at 24 °C
- [ ] Bingsu station stocked; soufflé cream in the fridge
- [ ] Batter: 3 original + 2 chocolate jugs
- [ ] Iced tea: 3 jugs of 5 L in the fridge
- [ ] Front lights on, cat sign shows **Open**
