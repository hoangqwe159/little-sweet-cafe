# Bingsu

This page has the method shared by every bingsu: the milk mix, the bingsu machine, sizes and packing. Each bingsu has its own card with its toppings.

- **Purpose:** Make every bingsu the same way.
- **Who does it:** Dessert station staff
- **When:** Every bingsu order
- **Time needed:** TODO(owner)
- **Equipment and supplies:** bingsu machine, measuring jug, shot measure, small whisk, cup or bowl, ice cream scoop, whipped cream dispenser, round container (Uber Eats)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-06

## Sizes

- **Cup:** 2 shots sugar syrup (60 ml) + 300 ml milk
- **Bowl:** 3 shots sugar syrup (90 ml) + 500 ml milk. The bowl gets **more toppings** than the cup.

## Flavour bases

Original and Oreo use **plain milk**, with no flavour base. For the other flavours, make the base first:

- **Strawberry** (Strawberry Bingsu): 2 tsp strawberry powder + 50 ml hot water
- **Chocolate** (Brownie Bingsu): 1 tbsp chocolate powder + 50 ml hot water
- **Matcha** (Matcha Bingsu): matcha powder + 50 ml hot water. Use **4 g (about 1 tsp) for a cup** and **6 g (about 1.5 tsp) for a bowl**.

## Make the milk mix

!!! warning "⚠️ Warning: hot water"
    Use a jug with a handle when you mix powder with hot water.

1. Put the flavour powder in the jug, if the item has one.
2. Add **50 ml hot water**.
3. Whisk until the powder is fully dissolved, with no lumps.
4. Add the sugar syrup: **2 shots (60 ml)** for a cup or **3 shots (90 ml)** for a bowl.
5. Add the milk: **300 ml** for a cup or **500 ml** for a bowl.
6. Stir well.

## Make the shaved ice

1. **Turn on the water** for the bingsu machine.
2. Turn on the machine and set it to **bingsu mode**.
3. Put the cup or bowl under the machine.
4. Pour the milk mix into the machine.
5. Fill the cup or bowl with shaved ice, as in the photo.

## Top it

1. Add the toppings from the item's card, **as in the photo**.
2. Put **1 scoop** of ice cream on top.
3. Spray **whipped cream** on top of the ice cream.
4. Decorate with **powder**, as in the photo.

## Takeaway and Uber Eats

Takeaway at the shop and Uber Eats are packed the same way:

1. Make the **bowl** size.
2. Pack it in a **round container**.
3. Put the ice cream **separately**.

## Extras

Customers can add extra ice cream, toppings or sauce. These are the same extras as the waffles. An extra is the **same amount** as on a normal item. See [Bubble Waffles: Extras](../waffles/index.md#extras).
