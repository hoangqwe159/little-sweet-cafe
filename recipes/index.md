# Recipes

Build cards for every menu item. Each card lists the ingredients, the steps, and a photo of the finished item.

Square is the source of truth for prices. These cards don't list prices.

!!! warning "⚠️ Warning: allergens"
    Each card lists its allergens before the steps. If a customer asks about allergens and you are not sure, **do not guess**. Ask the shift lead.
