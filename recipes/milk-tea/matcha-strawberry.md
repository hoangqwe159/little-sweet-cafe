## Ingredients (1 cup, 700 ml)

- Fresh strawberries: **3**, muddled
- Strawberry sauce: drizzle on the bottom of the cup. TODO(owner): how much.
- Ice: **1 scoop**, in the mixing cup
- Matcha base, the same as the [Matcha Bubble Tea](matcha-bubble-tea.md): **6 g** matcha powder + **1 spoon** Coffee-mate + **1.5 shots** (45 ml) sugar syrup + fresh milk until the mixing cup reaches **500–600 ml**

!!! danger "⚠️ Warning: allergens"
    - Fresh milk: **milk**.
    - Coffee-mate: check the label (usually contains a **milk** protein).
    - Matcha powder and strawberry sauce: check the labels.
    - If a customer asks about allergens and you are not sure, **do not guess**. Ask the shift lead.

## Steps

1. Make the matcha base. Follow [Powder base](index.md#powder-base) with **6 g** of matcha powder: ice and milk go in the mixing cup, then shake.
2. Put **3 strawberries** in the **700 ml** cup.
3. Muddle (crush) the strawberries.
4. Drizzle **strawberry sauce** over the bottom of the cup.
5. Pour the matcha base (with the ice) slowly on top, so it stays as a green layer on top of the strawberry.
6. Close the cup. See [Finish and seal](index.md#finish-and-seal).

## Before serving

- [ ] Strawberry layer at the bottom, matcha layer on top
- [ ] Cup is full and closed
