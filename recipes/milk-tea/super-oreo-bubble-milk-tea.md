## Ingredients (1 cup)

- Cocoa powder: **1 tbsp**
- Coffee-mate: **1 spoon**
- Sugar syrup: **1.5 shots** (45 ml)
- Fresh milk: TODO(owner) ml
- Pearls: **1.5 scoops**
- Ice: **1 scoop**
- Whipped cream: on top
- Crushed Oreo: **1 tbsp**
- Wafer sticks: **2**
- Whole Oreo: **1**

!!! danger "⚠️ Warning: allergens"
    - Fresh milk: **milk**.
    - Cocoa powder: check the label.
    - Coffee-mate: check the label (usually contains a **milk** protein).
    - Pearls: check the label.
    - Whipped cream: **milk**.
    - Oreo: **wheat (gluten), soy**. Check the pack for milk.
    - Wafer sticks: **wheat (gluten)**. Check the pack for milk, soy and nuts.
    - If a customer asks about allergens and you are not sure, **do not guess**. Ask the shift lead.

## Steps

1. Make the base: follow [Powder base](index.md#powder-base) with **1 tbsp** of cocoa powder (chocolate base).
2. Add **1.5 scoops** of pearls to the cup.
3. Add **1 scoop** of ice.
4. Pour the chocolate base into the cup.
5. Spray **whipped cream** on top.
6. Sprinkle **1 tbsp** crushed Oreo on the cream.
7. Add **2 wafer sticks**.
8. Add **1 whole Oreo**, as in the photo.
9. Add any **drink toppings** the customer ordered. See [Drink toppings](index.md#drink-toppings).
10. Close the cup (seal or lid). See [Finish and seal](index.md#finish-and-seal).

## Before serving

- [ ] Right drink and right toppings for the order
- [ ] Cup is filled to the right level
- [ ] Cup is closed (seal or lid)
