## Flavours

- **Vanilla, Caramel, Strawberry, Chocolate:** 2 scoops of that ice cream flavour
- **Mango:** mango fruit sauce. TODO(owner): how much sauce, and which ice cream?

## Ingredients (1 cup)

- Ice cream: **2 scoops**
- Sugar syrup: **half a shot** (15 ml)
- Milk: half a cup to mix, then top up to full

!!! danger "⚠️ Warning: allergens"
    - Ice cream and milk: **milk**. TODO(owner): ice cream brand, to check other allergens.
    - Mango sauce: check the label.
    - If a customer asks about allergens and you are not sure, **do not guess**. Ask the shift lead.

## Steps

1. Put **2 scoops** of ice cream in the milkshake cup. Mango: add the mango sauce.
2. Add **half a shot** (15 ml) of sugar syrup.
3. Pour milk until the cup is **half full**. Don't fill it more, because it will splash in the machine.
4. Mix on the **milkshake machine**. TODO(owner): how long.
5. Pour it into the serving cup.
6. Top up with milk until the cup is **full**.
7. Stir.
8. Close the cup. See [Milk Tea: Finish and seal](../milk-tea/index.md#finish-and-seal).
