## Soufflé pancakes

**Every serve:** **3** pancakes (Uber Eats: **2**), **3–4 tbsp** soufflé cream, **1 scoop** ice cream (none for Uber Eats), icing sugar on top.

**Batter for 1 serve (3 pancakes):** meringue of **2** cold egg whites + **1.5 tbsp** sugar + **½ tsp** corn flour + a few drops of vanilla and lemon; yolk mix of **1** whole egg + **1** yolk + **1.5 tbsp** cake flour. Cook **4.5 min** each side. Then **3–4 tbsp soufflé cream** on top.

## Milk tea

**Every cup:** **1.5 scoops** pearls + **1 scoop** ice + the drink to almost full. Powder-base drinks: the ice goes in the mixing cup with the base.

**Powder base (in the mixing cup):** the powder + **1 spoon** Coffee-mate + **1.5 shots** (45 ml) sugar syrup, then **1 scoop** ice, then fresh milk to **500–600 ml** (less milk when there are more toppings). Shake.

## Iced tea

**Every iced tea:** **60 ml** fruit sauce (6 pumps or 2 shots) + **15 ml** sugar syrup (half a shot) + **1 scoop** ice + tea to the top. Shake.

Flavours (the fruit sauce): Grape, Passionfruit, Lychee, Peach, Grapefruit, Mango. [Full card](iced-tea/index.md)

## Frappe

**Every frappe:** flavour + **2 scoops** ice + **1 spoon** Coffee-mate + **2 shots** (60 ml) sugar syrup + **200 ml** milk. Blend. Whipped cream on top.

<div class="lsc-quick" markdown>

| Frappe | Flavour | On top |
|---|---|---|
| [Mango, Grape, Strawberry](frappe/index.md) | **60 ml** fruit sauce | Whipped cream, sprinkles |
| [Matcha](frappe/index.md) | **6 g** matcha, dissolved in hot water | Whipped cream, sprinkles |
| [Oreo](frappe/index.md) | **1 tbsp** cocoa, dissolved in hot water | Whipped cream, crushed Oreo |

</div>

## Milkshake

**Every milkshake:** **2 scoops** ice cream + **15 ml** sugar syrup (half a shot) + milk (half the cup to mix, then top up to full).

Vanilla, Caramel, Strawberry, Chocolate: use that ice cream. Mango: add mango sauce. [Full card](milkshake/index.md)
