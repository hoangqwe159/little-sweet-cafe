# Bubble Waffles

This page has the method shared by every bubble waffle: cooking, shaping, serving and takeaway. Each waffle has its own card with its ingredients and topping steps.

- **Purpose:** Cook, shape and serve every bubble waffle the same way.
- **Who does it:** Waffle station staff
- **When:** Every waffle order
- **Time needed:** 3 min in the iron, plus fan time and topping
- **Equipment and supplies:** bubble waffle iron, cone mould, fan, ice cream scoop, waffle cone holders, paper bags, $0.50 containers, recycle bags
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-06

## Which batter?

The staff make both batters in advance. The batter recipe is not in these docs.

- **Original batter:** Original, Banana, Strawberry, Oreo, Super Oreo, White Strawberry, Matcha Red Bean
- **Chocolate batter:** Chocolate, Caramel Banana, Dark Strawberry, Devil, Rainbow

## Cook and shape

!!! warning "⚠️ Warning: hot surfaces"
    The iron is at 150 °C. Hold it only by the handles. Use the cone mould to shape the waffle, not your bare hands.

1. Heat the waffle iron to **150 °C**. Wait until it reaches this temperature.
2. Pour the batter into the **centre** of the iron until it fills **about half of the iron**. Don't use too much or too little.
3. Tilt the iron so the batter spreads evenly.
4. Close the iron quickly.
5. Flip the iron over.
6. Set the timer to **3 min**.
7. When the timer ends, open the iron and take the waffle out quickly.
8. Put the waffle into the **cone mould** straight away, while it is still soft.
9. Put it in front of the **fan**. Leave it until it **stops steaming and is crisp**.

## Dine-in

1. Stand the waffle in a **waffle cone holder**.
2. Add **1 scoop** of ice cream.
3. Add the whipped cream, fruit and toppings from the item's card. Arrange them **as in the photo**.
4. Drizzle the sauce over the top in a **zig-zag**.

## Takeaway

1. Put the ice cream and toppings in a **$0.50 container**, separate from the waffle.
2. Wrap the waffle in a **paper bag**.
3. Put the waffle and the container in a **recycle bag**.

## Extras

Customers can add extras ("Extra for Waffle" in Square). An extra is the **same amount** as on a normal waffle. For example, extra strawberry is 4 more sliced strawberries, and extra Tim Tam is 2 more Tim Tams.

- **Extra ice cream:** 1 scoop of vanilla, chocolate, caramel, matcha, strawberry, rainbow, or cookies and cream.
- **Extra toppings:** strawberry, banana, Tim Tam, Oreo, wafer, pretzel, brownie, marshmallow, Taro Smash, whipped cream.
- **Extra sauces:** chocolate, condensed milk, strawberry, caramel, Nutella.
